Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I save with OneShot AI? ▼

On average, users see 20-30% token reduction on optimized prompts. The savings compound quickly—with 10 prompts a day, that's thousands of pounds annually. Your actual savings depend on how often you use it and your current prompt quality.

Does OneShot work with ChatGPT and Claude? ▼

Yes! OneShot AI works on both ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) and Claude (claude.ai). The extension automatically detects weak prompts on both platforms and offers real-time optimization suggestions.

How does the token calculation work? ▼

OneShot estimates tokens using industry-standard methods (~1.3 tokens per word) and calculates cost savings based on real API pricing (GPT-4: $0.03 per 1K tokens, converted to GBP). The estimate is conservative and based on your actual prompt text.

Is my data safe? ▼

OneShot never stores, logs, or sends your prompts anywhere. All analysis happens locally in your browser. We only collect anonymized usage stats (with your consent). Your privacy is our top priority.

Can I cancel anytime? ▼

Absolutely. No lock-in contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel your subscription anytime from your account. Your access to the extension continues until the end of your billing period.

What about Mac/Windows/Linux support? ▼

OneShot is a Chrome extension, so it works on any device running Google Chrome—Mac, Windows, Linux, even Chromebooks. Full installation guides for each OS are included.
