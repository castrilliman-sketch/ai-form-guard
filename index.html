Questions, answered.

Will it block real enquiries?

No system is perfect, which is why uncertain submissions go to a review queue instead of being deleted. You can always see what was held and why.

Do my visitors see a CAPTCHA?

No. Checking happens behind the scenes, so real visitors submit your form as normal.

What happens to my form data?

Submission content is analysed by an AI model to produce each verdict. Read our privacy policy for full details on how data is handled.

What happens if I reach my limit?

We email you at 80% and again at 100% of your monthly limit. Your forms keep working while we sort out the right option with you, including tailored plans for long-term clients.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are monthly with no lock-in.
